Research Notes
Title of the Paper
LLM-TIKG: Threat Intelligence Knowledge Graph Construction Utilizing Large Language Model
Citation 
Hu, Y., Zou, F., Han, J., Sun, X., and Wang, Y., “LLM-TIKG: Threat Intelligence Knowledge Graph Construction Utilizing Large Language Model,” Computers & Security, vol. 144, p. 103999, 2024.
Introduction
Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) is crucial for identifying cyber threats, but most CTI data is unstructured and difficult to process.
Problem Statement
Existing methods struggle with unstructured data, require large datasets, and fail to capture attack behaviors.
Research Gap
Current methods fail to efficiently extract structured intelligence from unstructured CTI data with limited labeled data.
Objective
To develop an automated system using LLMs for extracting threat intelligence and constructing knowledge graphs.
Proposed Method
LLM-TIKG uses LLMs and few-shot learning to extract entities, relationships, and TTPs.
Methodology
Includes text processing, entity recognition, relation extraction, TTP classification, and knowledge graph construction.
Experimental Setup
Performance evaluation, fine-tuning analysis, and real-world dataset testing.
Evaluation Metrics
Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-score.
Results
The model outperforms traditional approaches and works well with limited data.
Conclusion
LLM-based approaches improve CTI extraction and enable effective knowledge graph construction.
